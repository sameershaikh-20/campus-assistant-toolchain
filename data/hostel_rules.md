# Nimbus Campus Residence Rules

These fictional rules apply to residents of the Nimbus Campus hostels for the 2026-S1 term.

## Curfew timings

- Residents should return to their residence block by **10:00 p.m.** from Sunday through Thursday and by **11:00 p.m.** on Friday and Saturday.
- Residents arriving after curfew should contact the residence desk and record their arrival in the late-entry register.
- An approved academic or medical exception should be recorded with the residence desk before the resident leaves campus, where practicable.

## Visitor policy

- Visitors must check in at the residence lobby with a valid photo ID and be accompanied by their host resident.
- Visitors may use common areas from **9:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m.**; residential floors and student rooms are not visitor areas.
- Overnight visitors are not permitted in student rooms. Residents are responsible for ensuring visitors check out before visiting hours end.

## Mess rules

- Residents should present their campus ID at meal service and follow the posted meal schedule.
- Takeaway is limited to one reusable container per resident; dining utensils and trays should remain in the mess hall.
- Residents should report dietary or allergen concerns to the mess supervisor before selecting food. The posted menu is the source for that day's offerings.
- Keep tables clear and place waste in the labeled bins.

## Electrical appliances

- Low-power personal devices such as laptops, phone chargers, and desk lamps may be used in rooms if they are in safe condition.
- Heating appliances, hot plates, immersion rods, and devices with exposed heating elements are not allowed in rooms.
- Do not overload sockets or use damaged cables. Report sparking, overheating, or damaged outlets to the residence desk promptly.
- Use appliances only in the manner specified by their manufacturer and disconnect them when unattended.
